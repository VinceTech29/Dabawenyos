## Overview

**Dabawenyos** is a civic technology mobile application concept designed to connect every resident of Davao City through a single digital platform.

The project aims to simplify access to city services, official announcements, emergency information, local events, and community reporting while encouraging greater citizen engagement.

This repository currently contains the project's UI/UX prototype, documentation, branding assets, and future development plans.

---

## Vision

To become the trusted digital hub of Davao City by providing one platform where every Dabawenyo can stay informed, report concerns, discover local opportunities, and access essential public services.

---

## Proposed Features

- Community Issue Reporting
- Official City Announcements
- Traffic Advisories
- Emergency Hotline Directory
- Local Events Calendar
- Barangay Information
- Government Office Directory
- Flood & Weather Alerts
- Lost & Found
- Community Updates
